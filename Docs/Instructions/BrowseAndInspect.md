# Browse and Inspect
## Browse
Browse page offers a list of public illustrations and your own illustrations. There you can search for illustrations and click one to open it for inspection. This page is just a bare bones of it all and doesn't support illustration count over 30 in the data base. You can delete your own illustrations when viewing `My Illustrations`.

You can find the browse page from the navDrawer by clicking the project icon at the top left corner or from the front page by clicking the `Explore networks and simulations`.

## Inspect
After clicking a illustration card on the Browse page you'll be brought into the inspect view where the selected illustration graph is rendered and you can interact with it. If your user has created the specific illustration, there will also be a button `Edit` to start editing the illustration.
